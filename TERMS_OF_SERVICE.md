## 2. Description of Service

Elvie is a free, non-commercial Discord bot designed as a utility helper for tabletop roleplaying sessions of **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay (WFRP 4th Edition)**.

Features include:
- Experience Point (PD) calculation for characteristics and skills.
- Reference search for talents, abilities, and rules.
- Rolling from miscast and corruption tables.
- Interactive mini-features (such as fortune card draws).
